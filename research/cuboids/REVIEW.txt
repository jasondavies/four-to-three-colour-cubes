Focused mathematical review of the all-cuboid argument
======================================================
6 November 2026

Conclusion
----------
No mathematical gap was found in the proof of

  F(a,b,c)=max{16a-3,4(a+b+c)-16,floor((24c-1)/5)},
  G(a,b,c)=max{abc,16a-3,floor((24c-1)/5)}

for 2<=a<=b<=c. The universal conclusion follows from the written proof,
not from extrapolating finite inventory tests. The research draft is ready
for manuscript integration and full formalisation after any desired
external review.

This is a focused second pass by the same assistant, with a fresh geometry
implementation and a standalone symbolic arithmetic check. It is NOT an
independent human or external-agent referee report. No novelty/priority
assessment or full geometric Lean proof was performed in this review.

1. Model and inherited geometric inputs
---------------------------------------
The proof uses the four-colour palette throughout, with exactly three
exterior colours. Reducing individual source varieties by corner containment
preserves edge availability as well. Each original copy remains distinct;
a solution of the reduced inventory lifts position by position.

The source-set/target-multiplicity distinction is respected. In particular,
a checkerboard that possesses both cyclic corner types of a D target fits
any one of its eight positions, but still supplies only one physical copy.
The D corner matching is exactly a two-pool Hall problem, giving all three
inequalities in (2.1).

The bucket lemma needs only presence of at least two of the three uuv types
on each bucket source. Eight sources have at least sixteen incidences, so
two types have at least four suppliers each; their union sees all eight.
This proves the required Q corner matching. That justification has been
added explicitly to the draft.

A fresh program reconstructs all proper rotations and source colourings,
without importing the construction's geometry. It confirms the 68-to-38
reduction, both sixteen-variety orientation classes, all four T varieties
per class, the twelve P varieties per class, all six distinct checkerboard
missing pairs, and the bucket and corner properties used above.

2. All three lower bounds apply to every target orientation
-----------------------------------------------------------
The one-orientation obstruction can use only Q targets. Its repeated-colour
bucket supplies every corner and the four monochromatic edges, giving
4d demands along some axis d>=a. The capacity 4a-1 therefore fails in every
orientation, not just the displayed shortest-axis orientation.

Checkerboard sources force every target corner to be tricoloured. Adjacent
equal-coloured target faces would violate that condition, so the target
has opposite equal face pairs. Every corner and every longest-edge position
then requires the pair on the transverse axes. The demand is 4c.
The balanced pool of K(c)-1 sources has at most
ceil(5(K(c)-1)/6)<=4c-1 suppliers of EVERY pair. Both the corners and the
long edges must be counted; omitting either would weaken the obstruction.

The fresh geometry program checks the target restrictions and orientation
balance on all 2,160 physical three-colour face assignments. The earlier
physical audit also checks both lower families against all 648 target axis
configurations on twelve representative shapes.

3. Weighted triangle selection
--------------------------------
The six numerical bounds in (4.1) are sufficient; no unstated bound on
aspect ratio is used. They now also have a Mathlib-only proof for arbitrary
natural-number dimensions and inventory size (weighted_bounds).

In the light-edge branch, assuming every good complementary triangle has
all edges above s_c makes the other three vertices good, regardless of
whether the original good vertex owed its status to ordinary sources.
Then every edge is above s_c, contradicting N<=6s_c+5.

In the heavy-edge branch, choosing an endpoint ensures goodness and excludes
the heavy edge from the complementary triangle. The other five edges have
total weight at most 4b-1. The smallest, middle and largest complementary
weights are bounded by one third, one half and all of that total. Sorting
these three weights is enough; it imposes no impossible axis assignment.
Every permutation of the three triangle edges is realised by a D target.
This axis-realisation explanation has been added to the draft.

The selected target may differ from the first corner-solvable D target.
After choosing it, reserve an actual matching of its eight corners. Those
sources possess all three required pairs. Each remaining edge pool has
its stated supply, any two pools cover every residual source, and the total
demand is at most N-8. This is precisely the required Hall argument.

4. Q and H reservations and residual Hall conditions
----------------------------------------------------
For H=(v,w,u,u,u,v), the corner and directional multisets in Section 5 are
correct. Four T_u and two T_v fill the six bicoloured corners; one additional
ordinary majority copy and the selected minority copy fill opposite cyclic
orientations of the tricoloured triple. Adding 2(a-2) T_u and b-2 T_v fills
all monochromatic edges. Thus the reservations total r=2a+b+2.

The largest bichromatic demand is 2a+b+2c-10. Adding r and the worst-case
three missing suppliers gives 4a+2b+2c-5. For Q, r=4a and the largest demand
is 2b+2c-8, giving exactly the same budget. The total demands are L-r.
Thus the displayed common bound controls all singleton-type and total
Hall inequalities. Sets meeting at least two bichromatic types see every
remaining source. Zero-demand directions cause no additional constraint.

The extra majority copy is not counted among the T reservations. Its
availability follows from A>=16a-9>=2a+b+1 in the wide case and A>=23>2a+b
when a=2. These explicit inequalities prevent reuse of a physical copy.
The research draft now spells out the disjoint reservations.

The independent physical audit checks 2,112 H pool-to-position incidences,
as well as the direction counts and whole-frame controls. ArithmeticReview
checks the common residual budget and extra-source inequalities without a
finite dimension bound.

5. Exhaustion of the no-D/no-Q branch
--------------------------------------
No D implies 4B+2C<=12, M=B+C<=6, and A>=N-6. No Q bounds each majority
bucket by h=4a-1 and yields N<=16a+2, n_T>=16a-14, and M>0.
These deductions do not assume that ordinary majority and checkerboard
supplies are disjoint after any earlier reservation: no reservations have
yet been made at this stage.

For a>=3, N>=L gives b+c<=3a+4. Two T counts below b would imply
n_T<=8a+2b-4<=11a<16a-14. A largest T count is at least 2a. Every minority
source supports a triple containing that colour, and at least one of its
other colours has the required b copies. This covers all wide boxes.

For a=2, b<=5 is forced. At b=5, the sharper n_T>=24 gives the same partner
argument. At b<=4, at least two colours have T count at least b. If there
are at least three, a triple through a largest-count colour has a suitable
partner. If there are exactly two, n_T<=20 forces M>=5, and both high
colours actually have at least four copies. A minority ordinary cube
supports their pair. If all minority cubes are checkerboards, at most
three can miss that pair, so at least one supports it. This resolves the
last case without a second H orientation or a hidden corner theorem.

The arithmetic in the minority, bucket, wide-box and two-high-colour steps
has separate universally quantified Lean proofs. These are arithmetic
checks only; the geometric upper theorem remains a written argument.

6. Completion and corollaries
------------------------------
The side-length restriction is essential. With every side at least two,
off-frame positions expose at most one face, so any unused source can fill
their prescribed colour. The volume exceeds L, by the displayed polynomial
identity, yielding G=max{abc,16a-3,K(c)}.

The four non-rod complete-box exceptions and the exact-cardinality region
follow with the stated integer endpoints. In particular the frame region
uses c<=5(a+b)-19, including equality; the standalone exact_cardinality
lemma checks this rounding for arbitrary dimensions.
