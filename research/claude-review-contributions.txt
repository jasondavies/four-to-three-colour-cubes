Claude review contributions
Recorded 1 November 2026

Provenance
This is a summary of reviews supplied by the author in the project
conversation and attributed there to Claude (Anthropic). The model version
and original execution environment were not recorded. It is not a new
independent review or a transcript of a tool run in this repository.

Mathematical contributions incorporated through subsequent Codex revisions
- Combine the smaller ordinary orientation class with checkerboards as
  minority cubes; count T and P cubes only in the majority class. This
  removes the separate checkerboard and mixed-orientation cases.
- Prove three-colour corner balance by counting directed colour transitions
  at the two endpoints of each cube edge.
- Identify the common signed trichromatic imbalance as a degree and explain
  its behaviour under reflection and colour permutations.
- Give a direct corner-assignment proof of the bucket bound using the
  types each cube misses.
- Shorten the threshold proof using the multiplicity 1-or-8 contradiction.

Further review contributions
The supplied reviews suggested improvements to definitions, notation,
ordering and concision; identified missing attribution and stale fidelity
statements; requested an explicit check of the printed corner-type table;
and clarified the remaining literature-access limitation. They also proposed
the merged strengthened-bucket construction, explicit minority support,
and the uniqueness corollary up to colour permutation.

Scope and limitations
The supplied reviews report mathematical and finite-geometric checks of
successive manuscript versions. Their claims should not be confused with
checks newly executed by the repository's scripts or with Lean verification
of the revised written argument. The Lean proof retains a related counting
argument and checks the lower bound by finite Hall deficiencies; it does not
formalise the edge-count balance proof or the degree remark.

The older GPT-6-Astra review in research/independent-review.txt covers the
original development before extraction and the later manuscript rewrites.
Neither that record nor the supplied Claude reviews is independent human
peer review, and neither establishes publication priority.
