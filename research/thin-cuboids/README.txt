Thin-cuboid integration and proof review
=======================================
6 November 2026

Scope
-----
This record describes the earlier integration of sharp thresholds for
2 x 2 x l rods (l>=2) and 2 x 3 x l strips (l>=3). The manuscript now
derives both from paper/cuboids.tex. The original strip-to-rod route
remains in Lean and in the construction scripts. The strip upper bound implies the rod upper bound
by deleting four short-edge positions; only the l=2 corner case is separate.

The worktree was created from clean commit 4dd7941, on branch
research/thin-cuboids. Concurrent uncommitted changes in the production
checkout were deliberately left untouched. The manuscript is a v2 draft;
this work has not uploaded it to Hexagon or submitted it to a journal.

Written review
--------------
A fresh proof pass found no defect. The points requiring particular care:

* The balanced-checkerboard obstruction counts the eight corners AND all
  long-edge positions, a total of 4l demands for one pair. It works for
  every target orientation. The four extra strip positions do not weaken it.
* In the D branch, two distinct edges in a complementary triangle share
  a colour, so they can be assigned simultaneously to long and short axes.
  The heavy-edge case supplies a new good vertex; the initial good target
  need not be retained.
* In the no-D branch, summing B+z_v<=3 gives at most six minority sources.
  This uses four distinct choices of the omitted colour, not four targets
  with independent supplies.
* The Q and two H orientations account for all noncorner monochromatic
  demands BEFORE Hall is applied to the remaining bichromatic positions.
  Their reservations use 8, 10 and 9 distinct sources respectively. The
  extra majority source for a trichromatic corner remains available.
* For the residual Hall matching, any two distinct bichromatic types see
  every residual source. Singleton-type demands and the total demand are
  therefore the full set of required inequalities, even in the three-type
  H-bb case.
* Length three satisfies the residual bounds using N>=29; for lengths
  at least four, N>=4l+5 suffices. Rod length two is the established corner
  theorem. All off-frame strip positions expose just one face.

This is an internal mathematical review and a separately implemented
geometric audit, not external human refereeing. Literature priority for
these new families has not been independently established.

Computational checks
--------------------
The audit programs generate determinant-one signed permutations directly,
independently of the quarter-turn generators and cyclic-corner model used
by the construction. They test compatibility by physical rotations and
matching individual source copies. Exhaustive local geometry is distinct
from the inventory controls: the latter are NOT an exhaustive upper proof.

* Rods: 274,176 source/target/position incidences, 336 oriented targets,
  986 positive inventory controls, and balanced lower witnesses at lengths
  2 through 20. Positive controls exercise D, Q and H.
* Strips: 705,024 incidences, 648 oriented targets, 1,140 positive controls,
  balanced lower witnesses at lengths 3 through 20, and controls exercising
  D, Q, H-aa and H-bb. The explicit H-bb inventory is saved in the report.
* Both check reduction transport against physical rotation compatibility.
* Every one-cube extension of the 28-cube obstruction is checked through
  length six (starting at two for rods and three for strips).

The target lists deliberately allow distinct axis assignments even when
lengths coincide. Duplicate physical cases do not affect completeness.
Reports are deterministic JSON and are regenerated by the commands below.

Lean coverage
-------------
ThreeColour/ThinBounds.lean proves the numerical inequalities, including
natural subtraction/division, and the majority/reservation counting.
ThreeColour/ThinSelection.lean proves long-edge propagation and the strip
pair-selection lemma for arbitrary nonnegative K4 edge weights.
ThreeColour/ThinResults.lean proves the sharp physical rod/strip frame and
complete-box thresholds as IsLeast statements. BoxModel counts all exposed
positions and proves completion; BoxMatching/BoxAssignment build distinct
physical placements. ThinGeometryChecks supplies local finite geometry,
ThinConstructions/ThinUpper prove the symbolic upper bound, and
ThinLowerGeometry/ThinLower prove the balanced-checkerboard obstruction at
every length. The rod upper bound restricts the strip construction.

ThreeColour/Thin.lean guards ten combinatorial and nine physical theorem
axiom reports. The new files use no unlimited heartbeat setting or
native_decide. They are imported by the default library. Their definitions
and endpoints are outside the original comparator challenge, so a replay
of that challenge does not cover the new extension.

Local validation, 6 November 2026
--------------------------------
The physical Thin target and all nineteen axiom guards passed with the
pinned toolchain. LEAN_NUM_THREADS=1 make check then passed the complete
repository checks and default Lean targets, including Solution. make paper
produced the fourteen-page PDF without unresolved references or box
warnings; the final page was rendered and inspected.

This record describes the thin-box validation before the subsequent
all-cuboid manuscript integration. No
fresh external Lean/NanoDa/con-ron comparator replay or upload is claimed.

Reproduce from the repository root
----------------------------------
  lake exe cache get             # first build, if needed
  make check-thin
  make paper

check-thin runs Python standard-library scripts and builds the new Lean
modules with serial Lean scheduling. Its first build also checks the
existing corner-theorem dependencies. The regular make check also runs these geometry audits and imports
the new modules via the library root. Before a release, freeze the submitted
commit and perform the usual exact-commit comparator replay.
